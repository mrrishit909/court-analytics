# Evaluation

Generated by `python -m ca.evaluate` on 6 synthetic court systems (seeds 300 to 305; 6,000 cases each) that the demo and the tests never use.
Every outcome is drawn from a hidden model, so the estimates can be compared with the true probability, which no real court will ever supply.
Read these as a check that the methods work, not as accuracy on real dockets.

## 1. Motion-outcome model

Trained on motions decided before 2023; scored on the 10,762 decided after (48.3% granted).

| | AUC | Brier score (lower is better) |
|---|---|---|
| Logistic model: motion, suit, judge's shrunk record, outcomes in similar cases, four case features | 0.741 | 0.2062 |
| Judge's shrunk grant rate for that motion alone | 0.727 | 0.2110 |
| Judge's raw grant rate (no shrinkage) | 0.726 | 0.2116 |
| Base rate for the motion type | 0.650 | 0.2308 |
| The true probability (the ceiling: outcomes are coin flips around it) | 0.788 | 0.1873 |

Calibration (expected calibration error 0.011):

| Estimated chance | Motions | Mean estimate | Granted |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0% to 20% | 1,178 | 13.5% | 16.2% |
| 20% to 40% | 2,703 | 30.5% | 29.8% |
| 40% to 60% | 3,419 | 50.1% | 49.5% |
| 60% to 80% | 2,900 | 68.6% | 69.8% |
| 80% to 100% | 562 | 84.4% | 86.5% |

By group: calibration error 0.019 for plaintiffs without a lawyer (1,623 motions) and 0.010 for the rest.

The 80% interval around each estimate contained the true probability 39.5% of the time. It is not 80%: the model leaves out things the simulator uses (the strength of the claim, fact-pattern effects), and an interval built from resampling cannot know what the model is missing.

## 2. Similar cases

For 300 cases per court system: the ten nearest by TF-IDF cosine on the facts. A hit shares the hidden fact pattern.

| | Share of the ten that share the fact pattern |
|---|---|
| TF-IDF on the facts | 100.0% |
| Baseline: ten random cases with the same nature of suit | 20.1% |

Facts are assembled from four sentences per pattern, so nearest neighbours are easy here. Real complaints are not written from a phrase bank.

## 3. Citation treatment

Word n-gram TF-IDF + logistic regression over followed / distinguished / criticized / overruled, scored on 4,000 sentences it was not fitted on.

| | Accuracy |
|---|---|
| Classifier | 100.0% |
| Baseline: keyword rules | 69.8% |

The keyword rules are tripped by sentences such as "The court does not follow X" (distinguished) and "cannot be followed" (overruled).
